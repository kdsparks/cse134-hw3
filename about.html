About me

Cadie Sparks

/keɪɾi spɑɹks/

Hello! My name is Cadie and I'm a computer science student at UC San Diego. I like figuring out how things work and seeing what I can build of my own.

My recent projects involve software, electronics, or some combination of the two. I enjoy bridging the gap between software and hardware to engage with and explore the world around me. Particularly, I'm interested in wireless communication, space and environmental technology, and conservation — and how I can bring the software engineering and web development skills I've been learning to these domains.

When I'm away from my computer I'm a ballroom dancer, a musician, a linguist, and a fan of storytelling in various forms. (Though I am often these things when I'm on the computer as well.) I think a lot about many things, and surely many of them will find a home somewhere on this site.

This site is my home base on the web: a place to share what I've been working on and what I've stopped to pick up on the way. Some pages will be a work in progress, documenting things I've tried, lessons I've learned, and what I'm still figuring out. Other pages will leave space for others' work that has left an impression on me. My hope for this site is that it continues to grow as I do.

Thanks for stopping by!
