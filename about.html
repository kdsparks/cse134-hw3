About me

Hello! My name is Cadie Sparks.

Studies

Currently I am studying Computer Science (with a minor in Linguistics!) at the University of California, San Diego.

Progress on my degree:102%

Interests

  • I compete in ballroom and latin dance with the UCSD Dancesport team. Any partner dance style, I've probably done it. I've been dancing for three years now and love to both lead and follow (and switch).
  • I'm an amateur radio technician, or as we like to call ourselves, a ham. My call sign is KO6LNE and I like to join repeater nets and do contesting. I've had my license since December of last year, and currently I'm working on a tinyGS ground station and my own MeshCore node.
  • I'm fond of language and storytelling. Sometimes for me this means watching my favorite musical proshots, or analyzing phonetics and syntax, or making a long-distance contact over the air, or any number of things. I often find inspiration and heartache in a lot of things.
